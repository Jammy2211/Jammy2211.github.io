# COSMOS-Web Ring image

Selected for the Natural Language & AI website page. It is a 90-pixel crop
of the COWLS JWST NIRCam RGB colour image of COSJ100024+015334, upscaled to
840 × 840 pixels with Lanczos resampling. No annotation has been added.

- Local asset: [cosmos_web_ring_rgb.png](cosmos_web_ring_rgb.png)
- Credit: COWLS / JWST COSMOS-Web, Nightingale et al. (2025), [COWLS I][cowls].
  The ring was discovered by [Mercier et al. (2024)][mercier].
- Source: [COWLS COSMOS-Web Lens Survey repository][repo],
  `M25/COSJ100024+015334/6_rgb.png`.
- The same image ships with the PyAutoLens Assistant as
  `docs/images/cosmos_web_ring_rgb.png`.

Use as an illustration of the scientific target, not as an output from
the AI assistant. It is a crop and upscale of the survey RGB image, not a
lens model output.

[cowls]: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08777
[mercier]: https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15986
